Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to SpotiDown ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our website (spoti-down.com) and our standalone Android mobile application collect, use, and safeguard your information.

SpotiDown is designed with a privacy-first approach: we do not require account registration, do not collect personal identifiers, and do not track or sell your personal data.

2. Information We Collect

A. No Personal Data: We do not collect names, email addresses (unless you contact us directly), phone numbers, or passwords.

B. Anonymous Website Analytics: We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) on our website to understand aggregated traffic metrics (such as page views, country-level geographic regions, and purchase link click counts). GA4 collects anonymized IP addresses and device browser types.

C. App Performance & Crash Data: Our Android app may send anonymous technical diagnostics and crash logs via Firebase Crashlytics to help us diagnose bugs and improve app stability.

D. Local Device Storage: Downloaded MP3 tracks, playlist metadata, and audio settings are stored strictly on your local device (in your Music/SpotiDown folder) and are never transmitted to our servers.

3. How We Use Information

  • To ensure the website functions properly and connects visitors to the purchase page.
  • To identify and fix software bugs, crashes, and performance issues.
  • To evaluate high-level website traffic and feature popularity.
  • To respond to your inquiries when you contact our support team.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics 4 & Firebase: For anonymous usage statistics and crash reporting.
  • Vercel: For global website hosting, CDN distribution, and SSL delivery.
  • Gumroad: For purchasing SpotiDown. Checkout takes place on Gumroad and is subject to its privacy policy and terms.

5. Cookies and Web Storage

Our website uses minimal standard cookies strictly necessary for performance analysis through Google Analytics. You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or notify you when a cookie is sent.

6. Children's Privacy

SpotiDown is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it immediately.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
